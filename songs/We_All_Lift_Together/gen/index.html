We All Lift Together

By Keith Power

A work song for a community that labors and endures together

Written for the video game Warframe, where it’s sung by the workers of Fortuna. The Solstice version was adapted by Raymond Arnold in 2019.

Performed as a singalong at Bay Solstice 2021.

The original recording is on Bandcamp.
